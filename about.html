Reliable Service, Lasting Solutions

A.S Matawalle Computer Business Centre Udawa

We are a professional computer training institute and business centre committed to delivering quality ICT education and reliable document & registration services to the community of Udawa and beyond.

Our centre combines classroom training with real-world business centre operations, so students not only learn software skills but also understand how a modern cyber café and business support office works.

Located at No. 9 Unguwan Daudu, Udawa, Gidan Alh. Sada Haruna Mai Atampa, we welcome students, job seekers, and clients seeking printing, online registration, and examination services.
