About A.S Matawalle

A.S Matawalle Computer Business Centre Udawa is a trusted institution dedicated to providing high-quality computer training and comprehensive business centre services. Located at No. 9 Unguwan Daudu, Udawa, we equip students with practical skills in Microsoft Office, Graphic Design, Internet, Typing, and more.

Our mission is to deliver reliable service and lasting solutions — whether you need professional training, printing, online registration, or examination PINs.

CEO & Owner: Shuaibu Abubakar (B.Sc. Chemistry, Federal University Dutsin-Ma)
